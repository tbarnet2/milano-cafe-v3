# MILANO CAFE WEBSITE - PROJECT BRIEF

## Executive Summary

Milano Cafe is launching a professional, modern restaurant website to attract new customers, drive online reservations and orders, and establish a strong digital presence in Austin's competitive dining market.

---

## Project Objectives

### Primary Goals:
1. **Attract New Customers** - Compelling visuals and messaging
2. **Drive Online Reservations** - Easy booking integration
3. **Increase Online Orders** - Seamless ordering experience
4. **Showcase Menu** - Comprehensive, organized menu display
5. **Improve Local SEO** - Rank for "Italian restaurant Austin"
6. **Clear Information** - Easy access to hours, location, contact
7. **Mobile Excellence** - Perfect experience on all devices
8. **Fast Performance** - Quick loading, accessibility standards

## Design Direction

### Visual Identity:

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #c41e3a (Deep Italian Red)
- Secondary: #1a472a (Forest Green)
- Accent: #d4af37 (Gold)
- Text: #1a1a1a (Dark)
- Background: #f9f7f4 (Warm Off-white)

**Rationale:** Traditional Italian colors convey authenticity and heritage. Green and gold add sophistication. Warm background feels welcoming and premium.

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Georgia (serif) - Traditional, elegant
- Body: Segoe UI (sans-serif) - Modern, readable

**Aesthetic:**
- Premium restaurant feel
- Clean, organized layout
- Elegant but approachable
- Spacious white space
- Professional photography
