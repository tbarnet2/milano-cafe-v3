Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take reservations?

Yes! We recommend making a reservation, especially for groups or during peak hours. You can book online or call us at (512) 428-6076.

Do you accommodate gluten-free diets?

We offer gluten-free pasta and pizza upon request. However, we are not a gluten-free kitchen. Please inform us of any allergies so we can best serve you.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We offer delivery and pickup. Order online through our ordering system or call us to place your order.

Can I host a private event at Milano Cafe?

Absolutely! We love hosting special events. Email us at cafemilanoaustin@gmail.com or call (512) 428-6076 to discuss your event.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday-Saturday from 11am to 9pm. We're closed on Sundays. Last seating is at 8:30pm.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes, we have several vegetarian dishes on our menu. Our staff can recommend options that suit your preferences.
