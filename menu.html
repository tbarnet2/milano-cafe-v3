Dietary Information

Gluten-Free: Milano Cafe gladly serves gluten-free pasta and pizza, but we are not a gluten-free kitchen. Please let us know if you're allergic to any of our ingredients.

Vegetarian: We offer a variety of vegetarian options. Please ask your server for recommendations.

Allergies: Please inform our staff immediately of any food allergies. We take this very seriously.
