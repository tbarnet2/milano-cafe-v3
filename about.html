About Milano Cafe

Our Story, Our Values, Our Passion

Our Story

Milano Cafe was founded on a simple belief: great Italian food brings people together. Our family recipes, passed down through generations from our homeland in Piedmont, Italy, combine traditional techniques with the freshest Austin ingredients.

We're not just a restaurant—we're a gathering place where families celebrate, friends reconnect, and culinary traditions come alive. Every dish is prepared with intention, every guest is treated like family, and every meal is an experience worth remembering.

For nearly two decades, Milano Cafe has been a cornerstone of Austin's dining community, honoring Italian tradition while embracing the vibrant food culture of our adopted home. From the moment you walk through our doors, you're part of the Milano Cafe family.

Meet Our Chef

Our Executive Chef was trained in Milan and refined through years of Austin dining innovation. Leading our kitchen with a commitment to authenticity and excellence, our chef sources ingredients from trusted local vendors, ensuring every plate reflects our values: quality, tradition, and care.

With over 20 years of culinary experience spanning Italy and the United States, our chef brings a deep understanding of Italian regional cuisines. Every pasta is made by hand, every sauce is simmered with patience, and every dish tells a story of tradition and innovation.

We believe Italian cooking is simple—great ingredients, proper technique, and passion. That's what our chef brings to your table, night after night.
